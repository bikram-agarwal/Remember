Remember is built around one simple idea: if you dismiss a reminder without finishing the task, it comes back. No important item disappears just because you accidentally swiped away a notification.

Mark the note done, and the reminder is gone for good.

<b>━━ 📌 REMINDERS THAT STICK ━━</b>
* Automatically restore accidentally dismissed reminder notifications until they are actually done. 
* Add up to three reminders per note.
* Choose from normal reminders, high-importance reminders with heads-up alerts, critical reminders that ring like an alarm every 15 minutes until done, or a quiet summary notification that keeps overdue and upcoming items pinned in your notification shade.

<b>━━ 🔔 NOTIFICATION ACTIONS ━━</b>
Add useful one-tap actions directly to reminder notifications so you can act without opening the app:
* Call, Message, or Email
* Get Directions
* Open a link, app, or shortcut
* Copy text or Share content

<b>━━ 💤 NATURAL REMINDER PRESETS ━━</b>
Set reminders and snooze with Timing (named times such as soon, later today, this evening etc.) or Duration (lengths of time such as 30 mins, 6 hours etc.). A custom date and time are always available.

<b>━━ 📅 RECURRING SCHEDULES ━━</b>
* Create hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly reminders. 
* Supports richer calendar-style patterns like "the last Friday of every month", with optional end dates or occurrence counts.

<b>━━ 📝 MARKDOWN NOTES AND CHECKLISTS ━━</b>
* Write rich markdown notes with headings, bold, italics, underline, links, and code blocks
* Text pasted from web pages, emails, or documents keeps its formatting. 
* Add pictures, attachments, and an icon from hundreds of symbols or the full emoji set. 
* Build nested checklists for groceries, packing lists, project steps, or anything else that benefits from structure. 
* Easily convert between notes and interactive checklists, or export notes as .md files (individually or in bulk as a ZIP).

<b>━━ 🔍 ORGANIZE AND FIND THINGS ━━</b>
Use tags, stars, archive, trash, full-text search, filters, sorting, and bulk actions to keep your notes manageable.

<b>━━ 📱 WIDGETS AND QUICK CAPTURE ━━</b>
Home screen widgets for upcoming reminders, starred/pinned/tagged notes, and quick capture. A persistent notification or the quick setting tiles can keep note creation one tap away at all times.

<b>━━ ☁️ IMPORT FROM GOOGLE TASKS, TASKS.ORG & MARKDOWN ━━</b>
Move into Remember from Google Tasks (connected account or Takeout JSON), Tasks.org backups, or .md, .txt, and .zip files. Drag and drop files directly onto the Remember window - including Remember backup .zip files to merge or restore. Task imports are read-only; Remember never modifies or deletes your external data.

<b>━━ 💾 BACKUP AND RESTORE ━━</b>
Export your notes and settings to a backup, optionally including attached media. Restore when moving to a new device, or import a backup to merge notes into your current collection.

<b>━━ 🎨 MAKE IT YOURS ━━</b>
Choose Material You wallpaper colors, hand-tuned color presets, your own custom colors & fonts, palette styles, gradient backgrounds, black OLED mode, cover images, and more. Theming is deep - the app can look and feel very different from any other notes app on your phone.

<b>━━ 🔒 PRIVATE BY DEFAULT ━━</b>
* Your notes are stored entirely locally. Zero tracking, analytics, or automated background telemetry. 
* No data leaves your phone without your explicit action (such as optional update checks or manually exporting a backup). 
* Lock the app with your screen lock or biometrics
* Mark notes Private (notification text hidden) or Secret (nothing shown in notifications or widgets), or hide note content on home tab.
