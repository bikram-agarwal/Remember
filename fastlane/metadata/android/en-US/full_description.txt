Remember is built around one simple idea: if you dismiss a reminder without finishing the task, it comes back. No important item disappears just because you accidentally swiped away a notification.

Mark the note done, and the reminder is gone for good.

<b>━━ 📌 REMINDERS THAT STICK ━━</b>
Keep reminders visible until they are actually done. Choose from normal reminders, high-importance reminders with heads-up alerts, or a quiet summary notification that keeps overdue and upcoming items pinned in your notification shade.

<b>━━ 🔔 NOTIFICATION ACTIONS ━━</b>
Add useful one-tap actions directly to reminder notifications so you can act without opening the app:
* Call, Message, or Email
* Get Directions
* Open a link, app, or shortcut
* Copy text or Share content

<b>━━ 💤 NATURAL SNOOZE PRESETS ━━</b>
Snooze with human-friendly presets - soon, later today, this evening, and tomorrow morning - without hunting for the right time.

<b>━━ 📅 RECURRING SCHEDULES ━━</b>
Create daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly reminders. Remember also supports richer calendar-style patterns like "the last Friday of every month", with optional end dates or occurrence counts.

<b>━━ 📝 MARKDOWN NOTES AND CHECKLISTS ━━</b>
Write rich markdown notes with headings, bold, italics, underline, links, and code blocks. Build nested checklists for groceries, packing lists, project steps, or anything else that benefits from structure.

<b>━━ 🔍 ORGANIZE AND FIND THINGS ━━</b>
Use tags, stars, archive, trash, full-text search, filters, sorting, and bulk actions to keep your notes manageable.

<b>━━ 📱 WIDGETS AND QUICK CAPTURE ━━</b>
Home screen widgets for upcoming reminders, starred notes, and quick capture. A persistent notification can keep note creation one tap away at all times.

<b>━━ ☁️ IMPORT FROM GOOGLE TASKS ━━</b>
Move into Remember from Google Tasks using a connected account or a Google Takeout file. Import is read-only; Remember does not modify or delete your Google Tasks data.

<b>━━ 💾 BACKUP AND RESTORE ━━</b>
Export your notes and settings to a backup, optionally including attached media. Restore when moving to a new device, or import a backup to merge notes into your current collection.

<b>━━ 🎨 MAKE IT YOURS ━━</b>
Choose Material You wallpaper colors, hand-tuned color presets, your own custom colors, palette styles, gradient backgrounds, black OLED mode, cover images, and more. Theming is deep - the app can look and feel very different from any other notes app on your phone.

<b>━━ 🔒 PRIVATE BY DEFAULT ━━</b>
Your notes are stored on your device. There are no Remember servers, no account is required, and no data leaves your phone unless you export a backup yourself.
