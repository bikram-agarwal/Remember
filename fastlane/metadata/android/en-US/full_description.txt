Remember is a notes, tasks, and reminders app built around one simple idea: important reminders should not disappear just because you accidentally swiped away a notification.

If a reminder notification is dismissed without being marked done, Remember can bring it back. Mark the note done and the reminder is gone for good.

<b>REMINDERS THAT STICK</b>
Keep reminders visible until they are actually done. Use normal reminders, high-importance reminders with heads-up alerts, or a quiet reminder summary that keeps overdue and upcoming items available in your notification shade.

<b>NOTIFICATION ACTIONS</b>
Add useful actions directly to reminder notifications:
* Call
* Message
* Email
* Directions
* Open a link
* Open an app or shortcut
* Copy text
* Share content

<b>HUMAN SNOOZE OPTIONS</b>
Snooze with natural presets like soon, later today, this evening, and tomorrow morning.

<b>RECURRING REMINDERS</b>
Create daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly reminders. Remember also supports richer calendar-style patterns, such as the last Friday of every month, with optional end dates or occurrence counts.

<b>NOTES AND CHECKLISTS</b>
Write Markdown notes with headings, formatting, links, and code blocks. Build nested checklists for groceries, packing, project steps, or anything else that needs structure.

<b>ORGANIZE AND FIND THINGS</b>
Use tags, favorites, archive, trash, full-text search, filters, sorting, and bulk actions to keep your notes manageable.

<b>WIDGETS AND QUICK CAPTURE</b>
Use home-screen widgets for upcoming reminders, starred notes, and quick capture. A persistent quick-capture notification can keep note creation one swipe away.

<b>IMPORT FROM GOOGLE TASKS</b>
Move into Remember from Google Tasks with a connected account or a Google Takeout Tasks.json file. Import is read-only; Remember does not edit your Google Tasks.

<b>BACKUP AND RESTORE</b>
Export your notes and settings to a ZIP backup, optionally including media. Restore a backup when moving devices, or import one when you want to merge notes into your current collection.

<b>MAKE IT YOURS</b>
Choose Material You wallpaper colors, hand-tuned color presets, custom hex colors, palette styles, gradient backgrounds, black OLED mode, card images, and more.

<b>PRIVATE BY DEFAULT</b>
Your notes are stored on your device. There are no Remember servers and no account is required.
