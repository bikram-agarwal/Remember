# Remember

<img alt="Remember: Notes, lists, reminders - done right." src="./fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/featureGraphic.png" />

Remember is an Android notes, lists, and reminders app built around one very
simple promise: important reminders should be hard to miss.

Most reminder apps fire once and move on. Remember is designed for the moments
where that is not enough: appointments, deadlines, medicines, errands, calls,
bills, and all the tiny-but-important things that should not disappear because a
notification was swiped away too quickly.

## Why Remember

### Unmissable reminders

Remember puts reminders first, not as an afterthought bolted onto a notes app.

- **Keep reminders until done:** when enabled, unresolved reminder notifications
  are re-posted if they are accidentally dismissed, and they keep coming back
  until the note or list is marked done.
- **High-priority reminders:** mark important items as high importance for
  heads-up alerts, vibration, sound, and lock-screen visibility.
- **Snooze that thinks in real time:** choose practical presets like soon, later
  today, this evening, tomorrow morning, or a custom date and time.
- **Reminder summary:** keep a quiet, persistent overview of overdue and
  upcoming reminders.
- **Action buttons inside reminders:** add actions such as call, message, email,
  directions, open a link, open an app, copy text, share content, snooze, or
  mark as done, so a reminder can help you do the thing immediately. A reminder
  to call mom can put the call button right in the notification.
- **Reliable scheduling options:** Remember asks for the right Android
  permissions so reminders are less likely to be delayed by battery optimization.
- **Recurring reminders:** set repeating schedules for habits, bills, chores,
  and other routines.

The headline feature is the accidental-dismiss safety net. If an important
reminder is still unresolved, Remember can bring it back instead of letting it
silently vanish. That is the feature this app is built around.

### Notes and lists that stay useful

Remember is also a full note-taking and checklist app, so reminders can carry
the real context you need.

- Rich Markdown notes with headings, lists, quotes, code, links, and formatting.
- Checklists with nested items, reordering, completed-item handling, and quick
  mark-done actions.
- Tags, favorites, archive, trash, full-text search, filters, grouping, and
  flexible sorting.
- Attachments and note pictures, including hero images on cards and reminder
  notifications.
- Home-screen widgets for due and upcoming reminders, quick capture, and
  favorite notes.
- Launcher shortcuts for creating a new note or list quickly.

### Make it look like yours

Remember has a deep theme engine because a personal notes app should feel
personal. You can keep it calm and minimal, make it colorful, or tune it into
something that matches your phone, your wallpaper, and your taste.

- Choose system, light, dark, or pure black mode.
- Use Material You colors from your wallpaper on supported Android versions.
- Pick from built-in color presets such as Forest, Ember, Grove, Honey, Ocean,
  Iris, Dusk, and Berry.
- Enter your own custom accent color with a hex value.
- Change the palette style to shape how soft, vibrant, neutral, expressive, or
  monochrome the whole app feels.
- Turn on a subtle gradient background.
- Use enhanced shading for cards that feel richer and easier to separate from
  the background.
- Enable progressive blur behind top and bottom bars on supported Android
  versions.
- Show note pictures as soft hero images on cards.

Under the hood, Remember uses a modern Material 3 Expressive interface, so the
animations, sheets, buttons, swipe actions, cards, and transitions feel fluid
without making the app harder to use.

### Private by design

Remember stores your notes on-device. Backup and import features are available
when you want to move or protect your data.

- Local-first note storage.
- Export and restore backups, with optional media embedding.
- Import from Google Tasks through account access or Google Takeout.
- PIN/device unlock support for protected access.
