Privacy policy

Remember (Android app: dev.bikram.remember) · Last updated: April 25, 2026

Summary

Remember is designed so your notes and reminders stay on your device. The app does not require an account for normal use, and the developer does not run analytics or ad networks inside the app to track you across the web.

Data stored on your device

Your content (for example notes, lists, and reminder schedules) is stored locally on your phone or tablet, in app storage. If you use optional features such as a PIN, biometric lock, or notification scheduling, related settings and tokens needed for those features are also kept on the device in line with how Android stores app data.

Optional backup and import

You may choose to export or back up your data to a location you pick (for example a folder in your own storage or a cloud provider you use). In that case, your data is handled by Android and the provider you select, not sent to a separate server run by the app for that purpose. If you use import from another service, that service will process your sign-in and data according to its own terms and policies.

If you connect optional integrations (such as importing tasks from another product), you may be asked to sign in with that provider. Only what is needed to perform the action you requested should be used, and you can revoke access from your account settings with that provider when available.

Permissions

Remember may request permissions you approve in system settings, such as:

  • Notifications, to show reminders and optional widget updates
  • Phone or dialer-related access, only if you use actions that start a call you initiate
  • Contacts, if you use features that read contact information you explicitly choose
  • Network access, for optional online features (for example sign-in to a third-party service you enable)
  • Biometric or device credential APIs, if you turn on app lock

You can allow or deny these in Android settings. Denying a permission may limit related features.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has provided information in a way that concerns you, contact the developer using the information below.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised, and the updated text will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions about Remember, you can reach the developer through the Google Play store listing, or the GitHub project, depending on which channel is appropriate for your request.
